# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (22 Nov 2026)
First packaged version: per-app mixer with FX, virtual audio devices and patchbay (swap/split/merge/mono, feedback check), Stream and Meeting mixes, Audio Unit plug-in inserts on channels and master, recording, scenes, MIDI, decks, soundboard, remote control (HTTP/OSC), Companion and Stream Deck add-ons, LoopNest ON/OFF toggle, Health page and Safe Mode, 34 self-tests.
Known issue: see STATUS.md ("OBS gets no sound from LoopNest Stream").
